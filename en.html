Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Harumo ("the app") stores your records and photos only on your device. The developer runs no servers and never receives your dose log, scalp photos, or notes. This policy explains what information can leave your device while you use the app, and how you can control or delete it. U.S. residents: see also our U.S. Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

Harumo is made by Wavefound, a sole proprietorship in South Korea run by Byeongchul Oh (business registration no. 604-30-10608). Contact: contact@wavefound.net. Person in charge of the protection of personal information (including for Québec): Byeongchul Oh, owner, contact@wavefound.net.

2. Information that stays on your device

  • Your medications (ingredient name, dose, start and stop dates), dose check-ins, dose changes, prescription dates, how-you-feel scores and notes, scalp photos with their brightness and face-landmark values, and app settings.
  • This information lives only in the app's private storage and does not appear in your photo gallery. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else, and it is excluded from your phone's cloud backups (Google backup and iCloud Backup).
  • Face landmarks (such as eye positions) and whether a face is in view are calculated on your device only, to help you take photos at the same angle and to time auto capture. They are not used to identify anyone.
  • For auto capture, the app reads your phone's tilt and movement (accelerometer and gyroscope) on the device, only while the camera screen is open. This is not stored or sent.
  • When you take a crown photo, the app finds where your head is in the frame with a model built into the app, on your device only. No photo leaves your device for this.
  • Photos that show your face are saved with a black bar over both eyes (on by default; turn it off in Settings > Cover eyes in face photos). The bar can't be removed later, and it stays in comparisons, cards, timelapse, reports, and exports.
  • In U.S. states whose laws require it (such as Texas), the app asks the App Store or Google Play for your age category and whether a parent or guardian approved, to follow those laws. The answer is checked on your phone and is not stored or sent.
  • Your dose log and scalp photos are health information, but we don't collect them. Comparison cards, reports, and Export files leave your device only when you save or share them, and only to the place you choose. You control those files. You can leave ingredient names off comparison cards. Nothing in the app is visible to other users.

4. International transfers

The recipients above are in the United States and Germany, so information may be processed outside your country, including outside Canada. You can refuse usage statistics and error reports and still use every feature. Ad-related transfers do not happen in the U.S. and Canada, or with Plus.

5. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: until you delete it. Settings > Delete all removes your records, photos, and settings from the device right away, and uninstalling the app removes them too. Deleted information cannot be recovered.
  • Information that left your device: kept according to each recipient's retention policy (usage statistics and error reports: about 1 year on PostHog's free plan). We delete the records for your Stats ID when you ask.
  • Switching phones: your records are not in the cloud, so they don't move to a new phone by themselves. On the old phone, use Settings > Export to save a file, then open Harumo on the new phone and choose that file with Settings > Import.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can view, edit, and delete the information on your device in the app.
  • You can turn off usage statistics and error reports anytime. To delete what was sent, email contact@wavefound.net with the Stats ID shown in Settings > About. We use that ID to find and delete both your usage statistics and error reports.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, Canada or U.S. states such as Connecticut), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or get a copy of your personal information, and to appeal our decision. Email contact@wavefound.net and we will answer within 45 days. If we decline, reply with "Appeal" and we will answer in writing within 45 days; you can then contact your state attorney general. U.S. residents: our U.S. Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains these steps in detail.

7. Permissions

Camera (optional: scalp photos), notifications (optional: reminders), saving to photos (optional: only when you save a comparison card), internet (ads outside the U.S. and Canada, usage statistics and error reports if you agree, update checks, source links, purchase checks), and vibration (auto-capture cues). The app does not ask for location, contacts, or microphone.

8. Children

The app is for adults. We do not knowingly receive information from children under 13.

9. Security

We keep no copy of your records or photos. Information that leaves your device is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and mention it in the app's update notes.
