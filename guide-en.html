Your first 6 months on hair loss medication, week by week

From your first photo to shedding, a first comparison, and your refill

Last updated 2026-11-16 · Wavefound, maker of Harumo

When you start taking or applying hair loss medication, it's common to see no change in the mirror for months. This guide walks through what often happens week by week, from day one to six months and beyond, and what's worth logging at each point. It's based on public sources from the American Academy of Dermatology and the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist about your own situation.

Day one — take your first photos

Changes are known to come slowly, over several months, so the photos you take now become the ones you compare against later. Take one of your crown and one of your hairline, in the same place, light, and angle each time.

Week 1 — it's common to see no change yet

MedlinePlus notes it may take at least 3 months before any change shows with finasteride, and at least 4 months with topical minoxidil. For now, logging every day is enough.

Weeks 2–8 — hair may seem to fall out more for a while

The U.S. label for minoxidil topical solution says hair loss may continue for up to 2 weeks when you start. If shedding lasts longer or worries you, talk to a doctor or pharmacist before stopping on your own.

Weeks 9–11 — time for a first comparison

If nothing looks different yet, that's common at this stage. Compare photos taken in the same place and light — lighting alone can make hair look thinner or fuller.

Weeks 12–15 — sum up three months

Showing your dose log and photos at a refill or check-up makes the conversation easier.

Weeks 16–23 — changes may start to show around now

Everyone is different. The AAD says it can take 6 to 12 months to see a difference with minoxidil, and about 6 months with finasteride. Don't decide from two photos; compare your first photos with your 6-month photos under the same conditions.

Week 24 and beyond — keeping it going

MedlinePlus notes that changes made while taking finasteride are likely to be lost within 12 months of stopping, and with topical minoxidil within a few months. Talk to your doctor before changing or stopping.

Tips for the same angle every time

  • Same place, same light. Dry hair.
  • Part and comb your hair the same way each time.
  • Hold your phone above your head for the crown; push your hair back for the hairline.
