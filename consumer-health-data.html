U.S. Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Applies from 2026-10-23

This policy explains how Harumo handles "consumer health data" under Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada law (NRS 603A.400 to 603A.550), Connecticut law, and similar U.S. state laws. Our general Privacy Policy covers everything else.

Who we are: Harumo is made by Wavefound, a sole proprietorship in South Korea run by Byeongchul Oh. We have no affiliates. Contact: contact@wavefound.net.

1. Consumer health data we collect, where it comes from, and how we use it

Category Source How it is used
Your records, kept only on your phone: medications (ingredient names, doses, schedules, start and stop dates), dose check-ins, dose changes, prescription and refill dates, how-you-feel scores and notes, scalp and face photos (photos of your face may count as biometric data), face-position points and brightness values calculated from your photos, and settings. We never receive this data. You, and your phone's camera and motion sensors Only to provide the features you ask for: logging, reminders, same-angle photos and auto capture, comparisons, timelapse, reports, and export. All of this happens on your phone; we can't see or access it.
Usage statistics and error reports, only if you tap "Send": a random Stats ID; which features you use (app opened, setup finished, photo set saved with the number of photos, dose check-in and how it was made, rewarded ad finished and where); error details; device model; OS and app versions; language, region, and time zone settings. Because Harumo is a hair-loss medication tracker, this can show that you use hair-loss medication. The app on your phone Only to count which features are used and to fix errors
Face-detection diagnostics, on iPhone only: the app's ID and version, a per-installation identifier, device information, and performance and setting values, sent by Google's ML Kit software while you use the camera screen. These can show that someone on your device uses Harumo. No photos are sent. Google's ML Kit software in the app Google uses them to measure and maintain its face-detection software. We don't receive them.
Update checks: your IP address and the time of the request when the app checks for a new version. This can show that someone at that IP address uses Harumo. The app on your phone To tell you about new versions, and to ask you to update when a version fixes a problem that could damage your records

We process consumer health data only as described above. We collect usage statistics and error reports only with your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

2. Consumer health data we share, and with whom

  • Processor (handles data only on our instructions, under a contract): PostHog Inc. — usage statistics and error reports (servers in Germany). Contact: privacy@posthog.com.
  • Third parties that receive data that may be consumer health data, each under its own privacy policy:
    • Google LLC — face-detection diagnostics from ML Kit on iPhone, and update checks through Google Play on Android. Contact: support.google.com/policies
    • Apple Inc. — update checks through the App Store on iPhone. Contact: apple.com/legal/privacy/contact
    • GitHub, Inc. — hosts our website and receives the update-check requests for our app version file. Contact: support.github.com/contact/privacy
  • Affiliates: none.
  • In the United States and Canada, Harumo doesn't show ads or start Google's ad software, so no data goes to ad companies.
  • We do not sell consumer health data.
  • No third party collects consumer health data about you over time and across different websites or online services through Harumo.

3. Your rights and how to use them

  • Confirm and access: ask whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy of it, with a list of the third parties and affiliates we shared it with and how to contact them.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off Settings > Send usage stats and error reports at any time. This stops future collection and sharing.
  • Delete: Settings > Delete all removes everything on your phone. To delete data that was sent, ask us (below). We delete it from our records and our processor's systems, and tell anyone we shared it with about your request.
  • Review and correct: you can view and edit your records in the app at any time.

How to ask: email contact@wavefound.net with the subject "Health data request". If you turned on usage statistics, include the Stats ID shown in Settings > About; we use it to verify your request and find your data. You don't need an account. Requests are free up to twice a year.

Timing: we respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we may extend this once by up to 45 more days, and we will tell you why within the first 45 days.

Appeal: if we decline your request, reply to our decision email with "Appeal". We will answer in writing within 45 days and explain our reasons. If we deny your appeal, you can contact your state attorney general:

  • Washington: atg.wa.gov/file-complaint
  • Nevada: ag.nv.gov/Complaints/File_Complaint
  • Connecticut: portal.ct.gov/ag/common/complaint-form-landing-page

We won't discriminate against you for using these rights.

4. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top, and mention the change in the app's update notes. We will ask for your consent again before using consumer health data in a new way.
